# Code of Conduct

RepoRadar is for everyone who builds or uses open source, regardless of background or experience.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers, and answer questions patiently.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not
follow this standard, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. Report
concerns privately through GitHub's reporting tools for this repository.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.
